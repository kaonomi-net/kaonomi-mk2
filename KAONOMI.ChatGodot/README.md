# KAONOMI.ChatGodot — the Kaonomi chat, EVE Online edition

The Kaonomi chat made with **Godot 4.7 (.NET / C#)**, in the style of **EVE Online**, with **every function of the
former default chat** (`KAONOMI.Chat`, Avalonia, removed since). Its view models, models and services are in `Shared/`
(namespaces `KAONOMI.Chat.*`, as they were): only the views are written in Godot. Avalonia itself runs headless inside Godot
(`Scripts/Eve/AvaloniaBridge.cs`) for the few view models that decode pictures or post on its dispatcher (its timers are
pumped at every frame).

## Run

```bash
cd KAONOMI.ChatGodot
~/.dotnet/dotnet build                      # builds the C# (also built by "dotnet build KAONOMI.slnx")
godot-mono --headless --path . --import     # the first time and after a new shader
godot-mono --path .                         # runs the chat
```

Options after `--`: `--prompt="…"` sends a first prompt (it waits for the server), `--command=/ontology;/relation` runs
commands (in the place of `startCommands` of config.yml, `/vision` by default: the commands run at every start; `[]` none), `--avatar=HAL` picks the 3D animation, `--voice` starts in the voice mode, `--live-chat` starts with **LIVE CHAT** on (it is off at the start; `--no-live-chat` is still taken), `--no-vision` does not open `/vision` at the start (it opens by itself when the machine has a webcam: asked to the system, `system_profiler` on macOS, without opening the camera; not when `--command=` is given, nor when the windows of the last close already hold it), `--back-after=2` sends the active
window to the back after 2 s, `--theme=kaonomi` starts in that theme, `--screenshot=<file.png>` saves what the chat shows after `--screenshot-after=<s>` seconds (8 by default; with `--quit` it then closes), `--no-restore` neither opens nor writes the desk of the last close nor the recent windows (a chat started for a test or a screenshot), `--themes` opens the screen of the themes, `--window-tree[=filter]` opens the tree of the windows, `--vision-source=<video>` makes the vision windows read a video instead of the camera (nothing of it is recorded nor sent to the search engine), `--audio-source=<file.wav>` makes the microphone windows (`/recorder`, the voice of `/vision`) hear a WAV file in a loop instead of the microphone, `--click=x,y[;x,y…]` clicks there after `--click-after=<s>` seconds (6 by default; in the pixels of a `--screenshot`; the next clicks 0.7 s apart), a point written `r:x,y` being a click of the right button and `s:x,y` a click with Shift held (a node of the paths of `/graphbase`), `--drag=x,y>x,y@<s>` presses the mouse at the first point at that second, moves it to the second one and releases it (some text selected in a test; where the selection ends is not exact), `--window-size=1920x1080` opens the chat in a window of that size (a test of another screen), `--type="text"` types a text after `--type-after=<s>` seconds (7 by default) in the field that has the keyboard, `--key=Right[,Right…]` presses these keys a quarter of a second apart after `--key-after=<s>` seconds (7 by default), `--no-intro` starts without the intro, `--no-services` starts without checking the services of KAONOMI (nothing is started, `/process` is not opened: a chat started for a test), `--no-kiosk` keeps the voice mode in the window without locking the system (a test; the login is still asked to leave it). The chat starts **in full screen on the smallest screen** connected (the one whose real surface is the smallest: the screen of a laptop rather than a wide monitor); `--largest-screen` starts it on the largest one, `--windowed` in a window instead, where it was at the last close. Close it with its window or `/bye`: a countdown from 10 to 1 over the whole screen (`ExitCountdown.cs`), the tick of a clock each second (a deeper one at 1), then the search engine is stopped (`POST /_shutdown` of KAONOMI.SearchEngine), then the Mongo engine (the `shutdown` command of MongoDB), then the chat closes (`searchEngineUrl`, `mongoEngineAddress` and `byeStopsEngines` in `config.yml`); Escape or a click cancels it.

At the start an **intro** plays over the chat, in full screen: the 3D animation of Kaonomi, already close to the
camera, comes a little closer and says "Jarvis is a douche" in English with a deep robot voice (Piper with its English
voice of a man, `en_GB-alan-medium` first, lowered and robotised), the words written under it. Its only sound before the voice is made by the code (`StartIntro.cs`, no file): a sliding
door of science fiction that opens by itself (a latch, a pneumatic hiss, the slide, the stop against the frame), over the
ambient sound of a lab of AI in a loop (the hum of the servers, the air of their fans, a soft drone, the chirps and
relays of the machines at work), which fades out with the intro. A key or a click skips it.

## Export: the chat alone, as a macOS disk image

```bash
cd KAONOMI.ChatGodot
godot-mono --headless --path . --export-release "macOS" ../dist/KAONOMI.dmg
```

`dist/KAONOMI.dmg` holds `KAONOMI.app` (Godot, the .NET assemblies published by the export, the native libraries, the
face images, the audio helpers, `config.yml` and `config.json` next to the assembly) and no source: `export_presets.cfg`
(kept out of git) asks for no script content. What the export needs:

- the export templates of Godot 4.7.2 .NET in `~/Library/Application Support/Godot/export_templates/4.7.2.stable.mono/`
  (`Godot_v4.7.2-stable_mono_export_templates.tpz` of the releases of Godot, unzipped there);
- `KAONOMI.ChatGodot.sln` next to the project (the .NET export builds through it);
- the signing by the `codesign` tool of Xcode, ad hoc (`codesign/codesign=3` with no identity): the built-in signer of
  Godot gives an app that macOS kills at launch on Apple Silicon (its entitlements cannot be validated).

The app is not notarized: on another Mac, open it with a right click the first time. The Whisper model, Piper and its
voices, and Ollama are not in the image: the first start installs them (see below).

## The first start

The first time the chat starts (and with `/setup` or `--setup`), a window checks its dependencies (`Shared/Services/SetupCheck.cs`,
`Scripts/Eve/SetupScreen.cs`) and helps to install them:

| Group | Checked | Installed from the chat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Speech to text | The Whisper model (`speech.model`), the microphone (PortAudio) | DOWNLOAD: the model from huggingface.co |
| Text to speech | Python 3.9+, Piper (`tts.piper`, run to see that its Python is still there), the voice of the language, an English voice for the intro | INSTALL / REBUILD: the venv of Piper with `piper-tts`; DOWNLOAD: the voices with `piper.download_voices` |
| 3D animations | The face images next to the assembly, the shaders, the renderer | |
| Services | Ollama, the chat model and the vision model, the Kaonomi server, the search engine, the Mongo engine, the data folder `~/kaonomi` | PULL: `ollama pull`; CREATE: the data folders |

What cannot be installed from the chat shows the command to run (COPY) or the page to open (OPEN). The output of an
installation goes to the log at the bottom, and the checks run again after it. DONE keeps the window from coming back at
the next start (`preferences.json`); CHECK AGAIN runs the checks again.

## The services checked at the start

Between the login and the chat, a popup (`Scripts/Eve/ServiceStartupScreen.cs`, on
`Shared/ViewModels/ServiceStartupViewModel.cs`) checks the five services the chat needs, **one after the other** in this
order: **SearchEngine** (`searchEngineUrl`), **MongoEngine** (`mongoEngineAddress`), **LLM Gateway** (`llmGatewayUrl`),
**graphbase** (`graphbaseUrl`), **Server** (`serverUrl`: the chat reads its projects, its presets, its voices and its
faces from it when it is built). One line each, with a dot (grey: not looked at yet; amber: checked or starting; green: it
answers; red: it does not run) and what is being done:

- a service that answers on its port is left as it is (`already running`);
- one that does not is started as `/process` starts it (`dotnet run`, or its binary when KAONOMI is installed; its output
  in `~/kaonomi/logs/<name>.log`), and the next one is looked at only once its port answers (`started in 4 s`);
- a service that stops just after its start, or whose port does not answer within 2 minutes, is told in red with its log;
  the next ones are still checked and started;
- the server is not started when the search engine or the Mongo engine does not run (as START CORE of `/process`): it is
  told `not started: it needs MongoEngine, which does not run`. The six agent hosts are not started here: a prompt that
  goes through the server needs them (START CORE of `/process`);
- a service whose process is found while its port is closed is being started from elsewhere (a terminal): it is waited
  for, not started a second time. A service of another machine is not started from here, and a viewer starts nothing:
  both are only told.

Everything running, the popup closes by itself (0.7 s; 1.6 s when a service was started) and the chat is built. With a
service that does not run it stays, with **↻ RETRY** (everything checked again) and **OPEN THE CHAT**, pressed by itself
after 20 s (a chat started with nobody in front of it still opens). **OPEN THE CHAT NOW**, shown while the services
start, does not wait: they go on starting behind the chat, and the status bar tells the result.

Once the chat is there (after the intro), the window **`/process`** opens (not for a viewer), in front of the windows of
the last close. `--no-services` skips all of this.

## The users

The chat opens on a **login screen** (`Scripts/Eve/LoginScreen.cs`): a login and a password, asked before anything of the
chat is built (no window, no microphone, no command). After a wrong password the screen waits before the next try (1 s,
then 2, 4… up to 30 s). At its top, a header after the LinkedIn profile of Jérôme Fortias: a band of the photo of a
talk (`Assets/jerome-cover.png`), his head in a white ring over it (`Assets/jerome-head.png`, a click opens his
LinkedIn page). At its right, a notice in English: KAONOMI is a prototype, used at the risk of the user alone (100% of
the risks), its publisher and authors giving no warranty and accepting no liability; then the MIT license (the login
asked to leave the voice mode does not show it). Under its fields, the banner of the YouTube channel **Le Lab IA**: its round logo
(`Assets/lelabia-avatar.jpg`, from its charter), « Jerome Fortias - Le Lab IA », @lelabia and a link to
`https://www.youtube.com/@lelabia` (the logo and the name open it too), and a link to the LinkedIn page
`https://www.linkedin.com/in/jerome-fortias/`. The users are in `~/kaonomi/users.json` (`Shared/Services/UserAccounts.cs`), readable by its owner
only: their login, their level and the salted hash of their password (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, 210 000 rounds), never the
password. When the file is missing or empty, the user **`admin`** (password `admin`, administrator) is created: change
that password with `/password` after the first login.

**The voice mode is a lock screen.** The 3D animation in full screen (the voice mode, below) is left only after a
login: a double-click on the animation or Esc shows the same screen over it, the login of the user filled in, with
**BACK** (or Esc) to go back to the animation. Whoever logs in gets the desktop and becomes the user of the chat (their
level applies from then on); the wait after wrong passwords goes on across BACK. Only the code that put the chat in full
screen takes it back without a login: an alarm that rang while the desktop was shown, `FULLSCREEN:OFF` of a `/remote`
script that went `FULLSCREEN:ON` itself; `FULLSCREEN:OFF` of a voice mode opened by a person asks for the login. While
it is shown, nothing switches to another application (`Scripts/Eve/KioskLock.cs`, `Scripts/Eve/AppSwitchBlock.cs`):

| System | Turned off | Left as the way out of last resort |
| --- | --- | --- |
| macOS | Cmd+Tab, Cmd+H, the dock and the menu bar (the options of presentation of the application), Cmd+Q, Ctrl+Tab | Cmd+Option+Esc (force quit) |
| Windows | Alt+Tab, Alt+Esc, Ctrl+Esc, the Windows keys (Start, Win+Tab, Win+D…), by a low-level hook of the keyboard while the chat is in front; Alt+F4, Ctrl+Tab | Ctrl+Alt+Del, Ctrl+Shift+Esc (Task Manager) |
| Linux, X11 | Alt+Tab, Super and every shortcut of the window manager (the chat grabs the keyboard, again every second); Ctrl+Tab | The text consoles (Ctrl+Alt+F1…), where the system has them |
| Linux, Wayland | Ctrl+Tab only: the compositor keeps Alt+Tab (said in the log) | |

Commands typed or spoken in the voice mode still run with the level of the user logged in (a window opened shows on its
stage). `--no-kiosk` (a test) keeps the voice mode in the window of the chat and locks nothing of the system; the login
is still asked to leave it.

| Level | May |
| --- | --- |
| administrator | Everything, with `/users` |
| user | Everything but `/users` |
| viewer | Look: the chat and the windows; the buttons that save, delete or publish (SAVE, DELETE, REMOVE, PUBLISH, 💾, 🗑) are dimmed and do nothing (`Scripts/Eve/AccessControl.cs`), and `/config`, `/setup`, `/process`, `/cmd`, `/killall`, `/json`, `/gateway`, `/remote`, `/preset` are refused (`UserSession.CanRun`) |

- **`/users`** (administrators only; refused to the others, with the reason): the window of the users
  (`Scripts/Eve/UsersScreen.cs`): each user with its level in a drop-down list, RESET PASSWORD and DELETE, and a line to ADD
  one (login of 3 to 32 letters, digits, `.`, `-`, `_`; password of 4 characters at least). There is always one
  administrator: the last one is neither deleted nor lowered; nobody deletes itself nor changes its own level. A level
  changed applies at the next login of that user.
- **`/user`** (everybody, a viewer too): your own profile (`Scripts/Eve/ProfileScreen.cs`): first name, last name, e-mail
  and phone (checked when given), profile (who you are, in your words), hashtags (separated by spaces or commas, with or
  without `#`, shown as tags as they are typed, kept as `#word` without twice the same) and a photo: UPLOAD PHOTO takes a
  picture (PNG, JPEG, WebP…), cuts a square in its middle, keeps it at 512 px at most in `~/kaonomi/users/photos/<login>.png`
  at once and shows it in a disc (your initials when there is none); REMOVE PHOTO deletes it; SAVE PROFILE keeps the rest
  in `users.json`. `/users` shows the name and e-mail of each user, and a double-click on a user (on its line, not its buttons) opens its profile in the same window as `/user` (one window per user, its changes shown at once in the list); deleting a user deletes its photo.
- **`/password`** (everybody): the current password, then the new one typed twice.
- **`/logout`** (everybody): logs out without closing the chat (`Main.Logout`). As at a quit, the windows with changes
  not saved are asked about first, the windows open are kept for the next login, what speaks stops and the microphone
  is released; then everything built for that user goes (the interface, the windows, the conversation, the alarms) and
  the **login screen** comes back. Whoever logs in gets the chat built again for their level: the services are checked
  as at a start and `/process` opens, but what belongs to the start of the application is not done again (the intro,
  what the command line asks: `--command`, `--prompt`, `--voice`…). Refused in the voice mode, which is a lock screen
  left with a login of its own. The buttons of a viewer are dimmed for whoever is logged in when they are shown
  (`AccessControl.Hook`, set once): a viewer, then an administrator after `/logout`, each gets its own.
- `--user=<login> --password=<password>` logs in from the command line (a test; refused, the screen asks);
  `--users-file=<file.json>` uses another file of users (a test never touches the real one).

What this protects: the chat itself. The login is checked by the chat, on this computer: the files of `~/kaonomi`, the
search engine, the Mongo engine and the server are not protected by it (whoever has the session of the system reaches
them, and can delete `users.json` to get `admin` / `admin` back). The level of a viewer stops the buttons and commands
listed above, not every way to change something in a window (a button named otherwise, a keyboard shortcut).

### The search engine as the store, a copy on the machine

`/calendar`, `/archi`, `/todo`, `/support`, `/obs` (the vault of the chat, `~/kaonomi/vault`) and `/entity:` keep
their **store in the search engine** (`Shared/Services/LocalSync.cs`, `SyncAdapters.cs`; on the server one index per
tool: `calendarevent`, `archiitem` (each fact sheet, each relation, the name of the repository), `daytodo`,
`supportticket`, `vaultnote` (each note, its id its path in the vault), `entityanalysis`; `PUT /synced/{kind}`,
`POST /synced/{kind}/all`), and **a copy on the machine**, their files as before (`calendar.json`, `archi/`, `todo/`,
`support/`, `vault/`, `entity-analyses/`): the windows work on the copy, with or without the server. Each change is
written to the search engine about 3 seconds after it; the whole of each tool is compared 20 s after the chat starts,
then every 2 minutes, and what another machine changed comes here (the window open reads it again: /archi, /support,
/todo, /calendar; /obs unless the note open has changes not saved). Each item carries a revision; what the machine
knows of each is in `~/kaonomi/sync/<kind>.json`: changed here only, it goes there; changed there only, it comes here;
changed on both sides, **the newest wins** (told in the log of the chat, « Sync »). A deletion goes the same way (the
item stays on the server, marked deleted; a note deleted elsewhere goes to the `.trash` of the vault here). A copy
that cannot be read is left alone (nothing is deleted on its account), and a copy gone (a new machine, a file deleted
by hand) is written back from the server. The reminders rung or snoozed stay the business of each machine. `/search`
finds the items (📅 🏛 ✅ 🎫 🗒 🔎) and OPEN shows them in their window; the server keeps a version of each at each save.

### Every save kept, and saved by itself

The windows that save a document in the search engine — the wiki, the mind maps, BPMN and infra, the CVs, the
interviews, the journal, the presets, the ghost writer, the ideas, the intels, the collection plans, the symbolic
patterns, the cache builders, the projects, the entities and the ontology, the analyses of pictures, the YouTube
videos — have **🕘 in their header**: the versions the server keeps of the document shown (every state saved), the
newest first, each with what made it (created, saved, deleted, erased, restored), when, by whom and the fields that
changed; selected, the document as it was then (the fields that changed in amber); **↺ RESTORE THIS VERSION** makes it
the document again (a new version: nothing is lost) and the window shows it; 📋 COPY JSON.

**Each change is saved by itself**: once a document was saved a first time (the first save stays yours: it gives the
id of a page, a map or a diagram from its title), **3 seconds after the last change** the window saves it, and the
server keeps that state as a version (the journal of the chat notes « saved by itself »). So every action that changes
a document can be traced and taken back. The interview saves itself quietly and without the summary by the model (its
SAVE still makes it); the ghost writer, the recorder and the moves of the kanban already saved themselves. The popups
that close on their save (annotation, note, task, cluster, relation) keep their manual save; their documents are
versioned on the server all the same.

## Differences with the default chat

- One window, no systray, no full chat on a second screen, no workspace panes: the desktop and its 3D cards replace them.
- No red strings between windows.
- The drag and drop between two tool windows is not possible (each window has its own viewport); files dropped from the
  Finder are taken by the window in front (pictures, journal, work).
- The music player reads its helper script from `KAONOMI.Chat/Assets/Audio` (next to this project).
